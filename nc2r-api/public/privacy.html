NC2R Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

NC2R ("NC II Reviewer", "the app") is a reviewer and quiz app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, provided when you register or when you sign in with Google or Facebook.
  • Sign-in identifiers: if you use Google or Facebook sign-in, we receive your public profile name, email address and a unique account ID from that provider. We do not receive your password, and we do not access your contacts, posts or friends.
  • App activity: your quiz answers, scores, lesson progress and certificate.

How we use it

We use this information only to create and secure your account, track your progress, grade quizzes and issue your certificate. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except with Google or Facebook solely to verify your sign-in when you choose those options, and where required by law.

Storage and security

Your data is stored on our server. Passwords are stored hashed, and sign-in sessions use secure tokens.

Your choices and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time. See Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

The app is intended for students and trainees and is not directed at children under 13.
