NC2R Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask us to delete your NC2R account and all data linked to it, including accounts created through Facebook or Google sign-in.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to jgumiran21@gmail.com with the subject NC2R data deletion request.
  2. Use the same email address that is on your NC2R account so we can verify it is yours.
  3. We will delete your account, quiz results, progress and certificate within 30 days and confirm by email.

Facebook users

You can also remove NC2R from your Facebook account at Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. Then email us as above so we can delete the data stored on our server.
